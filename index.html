THE PIPELINE

Three steps, zero modeling software

The brief taken from a viral build thread: produce a game-ready mech with one hard rule — no human ever opens a 3D editor. Every judgment call below was made by a model.

01

Generate

An image model designs the machine: armor language, proportions, wear. The render is the spec — there is no sketch phase, no mood board, no human art direction pass.

SYNTHESIZED REFERENCE
02

Reconstruct

TripoSR, an open single-image reconstruction model, lifts the reference into a textured triangle mesh — background stripped, geometry marched, vertex colors baked. About six seconds on a free GPU.

IMAGE → MESH
03

Ship

The GLB is decimated to 38k triangles with colors preserved, normalized to a ground plane, and embedded in this page. What you orbited above is the exact output — unretouched.
